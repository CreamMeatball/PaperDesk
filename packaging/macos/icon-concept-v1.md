# Paperdesk icon concept v1

2026-10-28. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Concept artwork for review; not yet wired into an application bundle. The PNG is preserved as generated, including transparency.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: first visual concept for a macOS desktop research-paper reader app called Paperdesk; one finished standalone app icon.
Primary request: iconic, elegant, black and gray only. Create an original, instantly recognizable mark rather than a generic document pictogram.
Composition: a single centered macOS-style rounded-square tile, front-on orthographic, large and filling about 88% of a square 1024x1024 canvas, transparent outside the tile. Broad smooth continuous corners. Spacious, optically balanced icon.
Symbol: one bold sculptural silver-gray folded-paper ribbon, forming a subtle uppercase P silhouette through its folded top and strong vertical spine. It should simultaneously suggest a turned page and a bookmark. Use only two or three generous geometric planes, with one deliberate negative-space opening. The fold is the distinctive identifying gesture. No separate objects, no document text lines. Legible and memorable at 32 pixels.
Palette and material: near-black charcoal tile (#171719 to #242426), cool graphite and soft silver-gray mark (#949498 to #dedee0), gentle tightly controlled tonal gradients, a delicate edge highlight on the fold, understated shallow relief and soft contact shadow. Modern precision, matte satin surfaces, tactile but restrained. Entire image strictly achromatic grayscale.
Constraints: original identity, no wordmark, no written text, no captions, no grid, no additional variants, no presentation mockup, no devices. Do not copy another brand's logo. No AI sparkles, stars, academic caps, circuits, brain symbols, magnifying glasses, excessive gloss, chrome reflections, neon, colorful accents, grain, or ornament. Produce a polished icon asset, not a logo sheet.


배포용 아이콘은 Paperdesk.icon의 네이티브 배경·배치 설정으로 원본 PNG를 재사용한다. 불투명 차콜 배경과 106% 레이어 배치로 시스템 아이콘 타일 안에 별도 회색 여백이 생기지 않게 하고 P가 잘리지 않도록 한다. 빌드 시 actool이 최신 macOS용 Assets.car와 이전 macOS용 ICNS를 함께 만든다.
